# Landing copy plan — brands + UGC/ad agencies

*Copy audit, Aug 2026. One big idea rules the page: EARLINESS. Every section either proves we're early or shows what you do with the head start. Anything else gets cut.*

**The one reader:** the person on the hook for "what do we shoot next" — a D2C founder/marketer, or an agency creative strategist carrying that question for 12 clients at once. Agency is the higher-LTV buyer ($599 vs $199); the copy must make them feel seen without splitting the page in two.

---

## Section order (change)

Current: Hero → Agent → **Marquee** → Pain → Curve → Intelligence → Niches → Decode → Band → FAQ → CTA

Proposed: Hero → Agent → **Pain** → Curve → Intelligence → **Marquee** → **Niches (becomes the brands-&-agencies section)** → FAQ → CTA

1. **Move Marquee below Intelligence.** Between demo and pain it's a capability flex interrupting the story (promise → proof → problem). After Intelligence it reads as "…and here's the breadth of the taxonomy you just saw."
2. **Retire Decode.** Its anatomy card duplicates Intelligence's beat rows; its spread card duplicates the Curve's cluster story; its sample data still says "Street interview · 41x" (stale taxonomy). Cutting a whole section is the single best tightening available. (If anything is missed later, the "TRENDING NOW" spread card can be resurrected inside NicheGallery.)
3. **Delete the Band water photo.** Full-width decoration saying nothing, and CtaFinal already has a beach image — two stock-photo bands cheapen both. (Odometer idea stays parked as its future replacement.)

## Copy changes, per section

### Hero
- H1 **keep**: "Catch the trend before it peaks."
- Sub — REWRITE (fixes the weak ending flagged earlier + names both audiences):
  - Before: "EarlyCue watches Instagram and TikTok at scale, spots rising reel formats in your niche, and helps you create your version."
  - After: **"EarlyCue watches Instagram and TikTok at scale, catches reel formats while they're still rising, and hands you the brief to shoot your version."**
- ADD one audience line under the CTA buttons (small, white/80):
  **"Built for D2C brands — and the agencies that run them."**
- NEW secondary CTA (biggest conversion lever on the page): "Book a call" is a high-friction ask for cold traffic with no logos to lean on. Add a low-commitment option in the hero and CtaFinal:
  **"Get this week's rising formats for your niche"** → email capture. It's a lead magnet that IS the product (an early-call email), builds the waitlist, and qualifies niche interest. Needs a small form or a typeform link — decision required.

### AgentPanel
- Swap chip 5 "Which hook fits my product?" → **"What's rising for my skincare client?"** (one chip makes every agency reader feel seen; the corresponding demo answer swaps "your product" → "your client's product").

### PainFeed
- Kicker/H2/punchline **keep** (H2 is the best line on the page).
- Body, one-word tweak for agencies: "Ad-spy tools call an ad proven once it has survived 30 days" → **"The ad-spy library your team already pays for calls an ad proven once it has survived 30 days."** ("your team already pays for" = recognition + sunk-cost sting for both audiences.)

### TheCurve
- Kicker/H2/sub **keep**.
- Step 03 body: "…the fit for your product, and the script to shoot it." → **"…the fit for your product — or your client's — and the script to shoot it."**

### Intelligence
- H2/bullets 1–2/punchline/CTA **keep**.
- Bullet 3, extend last sentence: "Pick one, ask for variations, or ask why. The agent answers" → **"…The agent answers — with receipts you can put in front of a client."** (Pitch-ready evidence is the agency's real product; this one clause sells the $599 tier.)

### FormatMarquee (after move)
- Label: "Every reel we track gets decomposed and labelled by format" → **"Every reel decomposed — hook, setup, payoff, CTA, sound — and labelled into living formats"**
- **Pills must follow the depth taxonomy** (current pills are the broad categories we've disowned). Mix structural names: "Propaganda I'm not falling for", "We listen, we don't judge", "5-to-9 routine cut", "Silent review", "Deinfluence stack", "POV: the group chat", "Day-5 duet reply", "Rate-my-routine mic run"… keep a few broad ones as visible *filters* ("Skit", "Tutorial") so the depth contrast reads.

### NicheGallery → becomes the brands & agencies section
- Kicker: **"BRANDS & AGENCIES"**
- H2: "Every niche, doomscrolled at scale." → **"Your niche. Or all twelve of your clients'."**
- Sub: **"Popular niches are tracked out of the box, each with its own radar. Agencies get a workspace per client — same tracking, same calls, and white-label reports your clients will assume you stayed up making."**
- (Keeps the doomscroll joke out; the tile grid already shows multi-niche breadth, which now doubles as the multi-client picture.)

### FAQ
- Q1: trim the duplicated survivorship copy (Pain section owns it now): **"They rank what already won. We track each format's lifecycle and call it while it's climbing — you move before the crowd, not with it."**
- Q "What do I actually get?": append **"Agencies get multi-client workspaces and white-label reports on top."**
- ADD Q: **"How much does it cost?" → "Early partners: $199/mo for brands, $599/mo for agencies (multi-client workspaces, white-label reports). Both come with a hands-on onboarding for your niches."** — pricing on page qualifies the call pipeline; decision required (defensible to skip while in discovery-call mode).
- ADD Q: **"We're an agency — does this scale across clients?" → "Yes — one workspace per client, each niche on its own radar, briefs and reports badged with your logo, not ours."**

### CtaFinal
- H2 **keep**: "Be early. It's the whole point."
- Sub: "If choosing what to shoot is your bottleneck, let's talk." → **"If 'what do we shoot next' is the question you're paid to answer, let's talk."** (names the one reader precisely — founder or strategist)
- Add the secondary email CTA next to "Book a call" (see Hero).
